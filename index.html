What you get

  • You choose

    Every request shows the topic, the context and the duration upfront. Say yes, or "not now", with no pressure.

  • Short and bounded

    One session is 45 minutes. No commitment, no schedule imposed on you.

  • It goes both ways

    Juniors can teach you back, whether that's AI video editing, new tools or new trends. You see what you'd get before you accept.

  • Simple to use

    Requests arrive by app or SMS.

  • See your impact

    30 days after a session, you get a note on how your help changed things for the person.

  • Your network stays yours

    After two years, your contacts remain yours, and you can keep mentoring freely.

What Relais is not

  • ✕Not a job, and not unpaid labor you owe anyone.
  • ✕Not a way of being pushed out earlier.
  • ✕Not a time sink: you decide how much and how often.

About us

We're a team of entrepreneurs designing Relais for a simple reason: too much experience leaves companies every year, and too many retirees feel their usefulness ended overnight. We believe retirement should be a transition, not a cut-off. Relais is how we're exploring it, and we're building it with the people it's for.
